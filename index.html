About Re-Hub

FREQUENTLY
ASKED QUESTIONS

Do I need a doctor's referral to book for a physiotherapy session? keyboard_arrow_down

No referral is needed. You can book directly with us — we’ll assess your condition and recommend the best plan based on your goals.

How long is your session? keyboard_arrow_down

It is a one hour 1-to-1 session.

Who are your services for? keyboard_arrow_down

Re-Hub is for everyone — from athletes to office workers, seniors to youth — anyone dealing with pain, recovering from injury, or aiming to move better and feel stronger.

What should I wear to my physiotherapy appointment or training session? keyboard_arrow_down

Wear comfortable, flexible clothing (e.g., t-shirt, shorts, or activewear) so we can assess your movement properly.

Do you offer packages or memberships? keyboard_arrow_down

We offer session packages and tailored programs. Ask our team about options that best suit your recovery or fitness goals.

Is Re-Hub physiotherapy packages claimable under insurance? keyboard_arrow_down

Yes — many of our physiotherapy sessions are claimable under personal or corporate medical insurance with pay and claim policy. Please check with your provider.

Do I need to make a prior appointment or walk in? keyboard_arrow_down

Prior appointment is needed to ensure there is available physio / coach to attend to you. Contact our team.

Can I do both rehab and fitness training at Re-Hub? keyboard_arrow_down

Yes! Our approach combines rehabilitation with functional strength and performance training — so you can safely return to activity and even surpass your previous performance level.

Can I bring my child / elderly parents for a session? keyboard_arrow_down

Yes — we work with clients of all ages, including children, teens, and older adults. Our therapists adapt care for every age group.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule my session? keyboard_arrow_down

No problem. Just let us know at least 24 hours in advance, and we’ll help you reschedule with no penalty.
